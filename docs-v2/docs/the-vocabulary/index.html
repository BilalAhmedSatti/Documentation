The Vocabulary

Every term in one place — what it means in plain English, and what it corresponds to inside our system. Rows shaded coral are pairs most often confused.

In brief — how to use this page
  • Three columns, three audiences. The term, the plain-English meaning, and the thing it maps to in software.
  • The third column is the useful one for engineers. Every concept resolves to a table, a posting, a state, or a port — not an abstraction slide.
  • Coral rows are traps. Terms that sound alike, get conflated, and mean different things — including pairs wrong in inherited sketches.

These words must mean the same thing in product meetings, compliance reviews, and pull requests. When two teams use the same word for different tables, reconciliation never finishes.

TermIn plain EnglishIn our system
SBP State Bank of Pakistan (the central bank & regulator) Primary regulator for Pakistan market packs, EMI regulations, and CCOF compliance
EMI Electronic Money Institution (Regulations 2023) SBP-licensed e-money issuer. Product is a wallet (e-money claim on trustee bank), not a bank deposit account
CCOF Customer Onboarding Framework (SBP 2025) Circular governing remote digital onboarding, identity verification ladders, Tat clocks, and Table-A data fields
BPRD Circular 04 SBP circular on digital onboarding & device binding (2023) Device binding, short-code auto-fetch OTP, 2-hour cooling-off, and NADRA BV requirement for mobile/email changes. Pulled into EMI digital onboarding via CCOF K
NADRA BV Biometric Verification via NADRA Finger, iris, or facial match through NADRA. Primary remote verification rung (Rung A). Required for J1, J13 upgrade, J14 contact change, and J19 cash-in
Verisys NADRA demographic particulars verification Demographic check against NADRA records without biometric match. Falls under Rung B (Verisys band PKR 50,000 load cap). Used only for SBP-listed genuine reasons
CNIC–MSISDN Pairing SIM ownership check matching CNIC with mobile number PTA / operator check ensuring the MSISDN is legally registered to the applicant's CNIC. Required on Verisys onboarding and SIM changes
Tracking ID Session identifier for an unverified onboarding application Issued at step 4 (APPLICATION_STARTED). Not a wallet number. Resumable for up to 30 days (J7). Retained on decline (J8)
Cooling-Off Period Mandatory 2-hour waiting window before full activation BPRD 04 A.viii constraint. Applies after onboarding, device bind (J9), credential change (J14), and limit upgrade (J13) before spending operates
Annexure-J Source of income document catalogue (EMI Regulations) Indicative list of income proof for PKR 1,000,000 enhanced band (salary slip, tax return, bank statement, rental/agri proof). Must stay in-house
EMI 14.VI Exclusions Load cap exclusions for salary, remittance, and utility payments SBP-permissioned exclusions sitting outside monthly load caps for BV adult wallets. Remittance cap up to PKR 1,500,000. Never applies to minors

AML, CFT & Risk Governance

TermIn plain EnglishIn our system
eKYC vs Screening Identity proof vs Sanctions list matching eKYC (Service 02/16) proves identity. Screening (Service 03/15) checks UNSC/ATA lists. Screening failure is a hard stop; eKYC timeout is MANUAL_REVIEW
CDD Customer Due Diligence Mandatory collection of identity, address, purpose, and risk profile. Must complete before wallet activation
EDD Enhanced Due Diligence Additional verification, video interview, and senior management approval required for HIGH risk customers (J6)
CRP Customer Risk Profile Automated risk scoring model (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) based on occupation, geography, PEP status, product, and channel
TFS Targeted Financial Sanctions UNSC / ATA 1997 designated person legal mandate: immediate freeze, service denial (J5), zero tipping-off
STR vs CTR Suspicious Transaction Report vs Currency Transaction Report STR is suspicion-driven (any amount, filed to FMU, zero tipping-off). CTR is threshold-driven cash report. Neither is outsourced
TMS Transaction Monitoring System Post-issuance automated rules engine detecting fraud, burst IBFT, mule devices, and suspicious patterns (J10)
MLRO Money Laundering Reporting Officer Named compliance executive who owns STR/TFS dispositions and FMU reporting
FTDH Fraud, Theft, Dacoity and Hijacking SBP operational incident reporting framework. Clocks are separate from AML STR filings

The traps, gathered

Tracking ID vs Wallet Number — tracking ID is an unverified session pointer; wallet number is issued only after complete CDD, pre-screen & cooling-off.

EMI Wallet vs Bank Deposit Account — e-money claim on trustee bank versus commercial bank deposit.

eKYC vs AML screening — identity verification versus sanctions list matching.

Wallet balance vs ledger balance — product view versus vault truth.

Hold vs settle — reservation versus final posting.

Available vs total balance — what you can spend versus what you own.

Quote vs transfer — firm price versus saga aggregate.

Control plane vs data plane — configuration versus regulated data.

Timeout vs CLEAR — fail closed means HOLD — never silent pass.
