07 - Risk tier written

Customer Risk Profile (CRP) scoring (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH). HIGH triggers EDD (J6).

07 Onboarding Required

CRP engine evaluates all compliance signals and assigns LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH risk rating. HIGH risk rating triggers EDD case (J6) and blocks activation until senior management sign-off.

Outcome when this step completes

Active risk_tier stored; HIGH risk routed to EDD workflow before activation.

Step 07 · Risk Tier Engine & EDD Gate

Presenter cheat sheet
  • CRP engine is deterministic: signals → LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH.
  • HIGH risk rating = J6 EDD case required before activation.
  • One active tier on customer record + history append.
  • Publish tier.changed.v1 so wallets and payments limit engines wake up.
