04 Corporate KYB Required

Compliance gate for KYB. The business is not trusted because documents were uploaded — it is trusted only after list screening returns CLEAR or a case is dispositioned. This is the same discipline as retail onboarding, applied to a legal entity graph.

Step 04 · Screen business & UBOs

Presenter cheat sheet
  • Say: “Every owner on the UBO graph is screened, not just the company name.”
  • Timeout is HOLD — if anyone says “the provider was down so we passed,” that is wrong.
  • Point at cases INSERT on the diagram — that is what the regulator asks for on a hit.
  • HIGH needs two operator IDs before disposition — not one analyst closing their own hit.
