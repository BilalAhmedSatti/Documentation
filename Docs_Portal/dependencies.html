Why this matters for scheduling

One chain decides the go-live date

Sprint 0 \u2192 Control Plane \u2192 Ledger \u2192 Pricing + FX \u2192 Payments + Payout rail \u2192 go-live. Everything else in the graph above has slack \u2014 it's necessary, but a delay there doesn't move the date the way a delay on this chain does.

  • External certification (NADRA, Raast, SBP) is tracked separately because it can dominate the date regardless of engineering progress.
  • E-Signature (26) must be built before Credit Bureau (24) \u2014 a legal ordering, not an architectural preference.
