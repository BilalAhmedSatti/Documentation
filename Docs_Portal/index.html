How it's organized

One core, two applications, three markets

The regulated plumbing \u2014 identity, screening, payments, tax, signatures \u2014 is built once as a shared adapter layer. Each application owns only what's genuinely its own.

A

Two applications

Digital Banking & Remittance owns the ledger, payments sagas and corridors. Takaful owns policy administration, underwriting, claims and the participant pool. Neither reimplements the other's plumbing.

See how they're structured \u2192
S

Shared adapter platform

Identity/KYC, Screening/AML, Credit Bureau, Payment Rails, Country Tax, E-Signature, Notification and the Control Plane serve both apps behind stable ports \u2014 designed once, certified once.
